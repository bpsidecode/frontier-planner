Controls

  • Pick a building on the left, then click to place it. Drag to place several (handy for roads).
  • R / Tab: rotate. Esc / right-click: stop placing.
  • Click a building to select it and drag to move it. Del deletes it.
  • Scroll to zoom. Drag empty ground, right/middle-drag, or Space+drag to pan.
  • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z: undo / redo. H: heatmap. G: grid.
